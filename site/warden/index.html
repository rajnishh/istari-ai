What it does

  • 01

    Services that stay up

    Dev servers, workers, databases and tunnels restart on their own, with health checks from inside and outside.

  • 02

    Heat and CPU

    When the Mac runs hot, the busiest programs move to background priority, then come back once it's calm.

  • 03

    Memory guards

    Alerts on memory pressure and swap that name the biggest programs, and who started them.

  • 04

    Made for AI agents

    Idle local AI servers and unused simulators are stopped, and every stop is written down with its reason.

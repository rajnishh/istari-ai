Warden changelog

Every release, newest first. Download: istari-ai.dev/warden. Installed copies update themselves.

#Unreleased (1.3.0)

#1.2.2 · 26 November 2026

  • Removing a merged worktree moves files git ignores, like .env or output/, to the Trash; its code stays in git.
  • Warden empties what it put in the Trash after 30 days (7 when the disk is nearly full), with a notice first.
  • A worktree a program has files open in, or one of Warden's services runs from, is never removed.

#1.2.1 · 23 November 2026

  • AI Agents opens in seconds, even with tens of thousands of conversations.
  • Conversations in every Claude Code config folder are listed, not only ~/.claude.
  • Resume uses the right config folder.
  • Claude Code's own cleanup setting is read per config folder.
  • Each tool's sessions appear as soon as they're read.
  • Big folders list the newest 300 conversations, with Show all and search.
  • Folders on a network share no longer hold up the session list.
  • Services: "Nothing on watch yet" is centred.

#1.2.0 · 23 November 2026

  • Warns before it stops something you may be using, with Keep It Running and Do It Now.
  • Urgent alerts (close to freezing, disk under 3 GB, a service down) come with a sound and a window that stays on top.
  • Menu bar: a calmer icon, the problem itself as the first line, and Pause Automatic Actions.
  • Settings in six tabs, and a Settings window of its own from the menu bar.
  • AI Agents in three tabs, with sessions as a tree by project and worktree.
  • Tidying counts only days you use the Mac, and lists what's due as Tidying soon first, with Keep.
  • Longer defaults: sessions archived after 30 days, merged worktrees removed after 14.
  • Away mode holds all tidying until you're back.
  • Warns 5 days before Claude Code deletes its own old conversations, with Keep for a year.
  • Says who turned a service on or off.
  • Unused simulators wait 20 minutes before being shut down, unless the Mac is close to freezing.
  • Services in one list, grouped into stacks, with memory per service.
  • Services can be Docker containers, start after one another, or run only when you start them.
  • Updating or restarting Warden never stops a service.
  • Spotted on this Mac: containers and servers Warden doesn't run, listed so you can manage them.
  • Take services off watch, one or many at a time.
  • warden services and warden start|stop|restart <name> in Terminal.
  • Disk Insight: what grew lately, which app wrote it, and what clearing it costs.
  • Rules for known disk hogs (iPhone symbols, browser copies, Simulator caches and more), updatable without a new Warden.
  • Low-disk alerts name what filled the disk, and go by space that's really free.
  • Sizes say what deleting really frees.
  • Clears spare Next.js builds, Cursor checkpoints, Flutter build output and Xcode builds of deleted projects.
  • Simulators, iPhone support files and a waiting macOS update are listed one by one, with Delete.
  • Settings → Privacy: what Warden connects to, a switch for each, Offline mode and a log of connections.
  • PRIVACY.md and SECURITY.md published.
  • Stops are reported when they're really done, with Force stop if one hangs.
  • One big local AI model at a time: an idle Ollama model is unloaded when a second big one runs.
  • Alerts when Docker, OrbStack or Colima goes down, and can start it again.
  • AI models are never cleared as caches.
  • The Activity page opens without jumping.
  • Calmer screens: actions appear on hover, and long explanations sit behind ⓘ.
  • Only one helper runs at a time.
  • warden version, and a mistyped command prints the list of commands.

#1.1.0 · 14 November 2026

  • The disk is watched in the background, with a forecast of when it fills.
  • Finished coding worktrees give back their packages and build output.
  • Build output for every major language, offered only when its tool's own file proves it.
  • Merged worktrees can be removed, after a memory card is saved (opt-in).
  • Memory cards for every merged worktree, with Reopen.
  • Memory cards for main checkouts, branches and plain folders too.
  • A folder open in your editor counts as in use.
  • Mercurial and Subversion as well as git.
  • Installers you've already used can go to the Trash (off by default).
  • warden disk shows where space goes and changes nothing.
  • The menu bar opens at once, even when the Mac is close to freezing.
  • Free memory now offers real choices once the safe steps are done.
  • A service already running in Terminal is adopted, not started twice.
  • About 8× fewer wakeups, half the CPU, and 20–30 MB of memory.
  • When version control can't answer, nothing is offered for clearing.
  • Fixes: a crash in Free memory now, a helper left stopped after an update, and others.

#1.0.0 · 12 November 2026

  • First run: you choose what Warden does on its own; anything that stops or deletes starts off.
  • Services: keeps dev servers, workers and tunnels running, with health checks.
  • Heat and CPU: the busiest programs move to background priority while the Mac is hot.
  • Memory: alerts that name the biggest programs, and acting at the first sign of a freeze.
  • AI agents: idle AI servers, unused simulators and leftover servers are stopped, and written to STOPPED-BY-WARDEN.md.
  • Stale work: sessions, apps and windows left open and unused.
  • Disk: caches cleared when space is low, and what's safe to free.
  • Docker, OrbStack and Colima: containers and what they hold.
  • Activity: charts of CPU, memory, swap and disk, and what caused each spike.
  • Alerts for apps not responding.
  • warden status, why, cool and free in Terminal and Siri.
  • Updates through Sparkle.
